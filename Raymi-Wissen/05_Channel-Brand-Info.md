## Why It's Written This Way (reusable principles for new copy)

- First ~100 characters (before YouTube's "...more" truncation) must carry
  the full pitch alone: concept + two personality hooks.
- Bullet format is scannable in 3 seconds — most visitors skim.
- Odd, specific details (salmon best friend, tako-ears correction, mascot
  Sumi) are chosen deliberately as shareable/quotable hooks — generic bios
  don't get quoted because there's nothing specific to quote.
- Closing CTA stays a single, low-friction ask (subscribe + say hi), never
  stacked asks.
- Hashtags: genuinely descriptive/searchable only, no padding.
